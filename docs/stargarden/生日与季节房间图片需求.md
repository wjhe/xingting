# 生日插画与四季 / 夜晚房间：需求与提示词

> 2026-11-05。供负责生成图片的智能体或同事执行。
> 背景：上一批生日图画了父母与弟妹，与设定冲突，全部未采用；四季与夜晚房间需要保持原构图，改用图生图制作。见 `图片接入记录.md`。

## 一、通用要求

- 画风基准：`art-src/stargarden/style-tests/cel-v3.png`，1990 年代日式赛璐璐手绘：细棕色轮廓线、平涂、每种材质一层硬边阴影、明亮干净的米白 / 鼠尾草绿 / 灰玫瑰色调。
- 每项生成 2 张候选，命名 `<编号>-1.png`、`<编号>-2.png`，放在 `art-src/stargarden/requirements-v2/candidates/`。
- 每次生成追加记录到 `art-src/generation_log.jsonl`：文件、模型、接口、尺寸、完整提示词、底图（图生图时）、重绘强度、时间。
- 不得出现文字、水印、logo；不得出现画面要求以外的人物。

### 统一画风前缀（英文提示词时放在最前面）

```
Original early 1990s Japanese fantasy daughter-raising game cel illustration. Fine warm brown hand-drawn contours, flat opaque local colors, exactly one crisp shadow tone per material. Bright clean fresh ivory, sage, dusty rose palette. No glossy modern anime, no gradients, no watercolor, no texture grain, no sepia, no text, no watermark. Restrained small amber-brown eyes, natural face.
```

### 女儿外形（画到艾莉时接在前缀后）

## 二、生日插画（文生图，3 项 × 2 候选）

### 2.1 设定约束（必须遵守）

- **家里只有艾莉和一位养育者**。养育者可能是爸爸也可能是妈妈，所以**画面里不能出现养育者的脸和身体**，最多出现一只从画面边缘伸进来的手，袖子用中性的米色或灰绿色毛衣袖，**不能看出性别**。
- **不出现其他任何人**：没有父母二人、没有兄弟姐妹、没有朋友或宾客。
- 地点是家里，温馨的小型庆祝，不是派对。
- 蜡烛**不要画成具体数量**（一局里每年都会复用同一张），写成“一支星形蜡烛”最安全。
- 呼应剧情的小细节（可选，不要喧宾夺主）：桌角放着那本绿色的星庭手账；窗外是星庭小城。

### 2.2 规格

| 编号 | 年龄段 | 用在 | 尺寸 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `birthday-child` | 11–13 岁生日 | 生日结算演出、月度总结 | 1536×1024 |
| `birthday-teen` | 14–15 岁生日 | 同上 | 1536×1024 |
| `birthday-grown` | 16–18 岁生日 | 同上 | 1536×1024 |

### 2.3 提示词（前缀 + 女儿外形 + 下列内容）

**负面要求（统一）**：`no other people, no parents, no siblings, no guests, no adult face, no adult body, only one hand at most, no multiple candles with countable numbers, no text on the cake, no party hats, no balloons crowd`

`birthday-child`

```
She is 11 years old, age-appropriate child proportions, wearing an ivory puff-sleeve blouse and sage pinafore with a small dusty rose bow. A cozy small home birthday at a wooden table by the window in the evening, warm lamp light. In front of her is a small round honey cake with cream and berries and one single star-shaped candle. She leans forward with eyes gently closed and hands clasped, about to make a wish, a shy happy smile. From the right edge of the frame, only one kind adult hand in a plain oatmeal knit sleeve gently places a small gift box wrapped in sage paper with a ribbon on the table; no adult face or body visible. A little green journal rests at the corner of the table. Through the window, the lights of a small European fantasy town. Warm, intimate, quiet, just the two of them.
```

`birthday-teen`

```
She is 14 years old, natural adolescent proportions, wearing an ivory blouse and sage dress. A cozy home birthday at a wooden table by the window in the evening, soft warm lamp light. A round honey cake with cream and berries and one single star-shaped candle glowing. She holds the knife to cut the first slice and looks up toward the viewer with a happy, slightly embarrassed smile. From the left edge of the frame, only one adult hand in a plain oatmeal knit sleeve offers a small wrapped gift with a dusty rose ribbon; no adult face or body visible. A green journal and a teapot on the table. Small town lights outside the window. Warm and gentle, just the two of them.
```

`birthday-grown`

```
She is 17 years old, clearly an older adolescent young woman with older facial proportions, wearing an ivory high-collar blouse and sage dress. A quiet home birthday at a wooden table by the window in the evening, warm lamp light. A round honey cake with one single star-shaped candle. She holds a small opened gift in both hands, a simple silver key-shaped pendant on a ribbon, and looks toward the viewer with a grateful, tender smile. From the right edge of the frame, only one adult hand in a plain oatmeal knit sleeve rests on the table near her; no adult face or body visible. The green journal lies open beside the cake. Town lights and the first stars outside the window. Warm, grown-up, a little emotional, just the two of them.
```

> 18 岁礼物里的“钥匙吊坠”呼应主线“交给你的钥匙”。如果剧情侧另有安排，可改成“一束小花”。

### 2.4 验收

- [ ] 画面里只有艾莉，最多一只手；手和袖子看不出性别
- [ ] 没有其他人物，包括窗外和背景
- [ ] 只有一支星形蜡烛，蛋糕上没有文字
- [ ] 三个年龄段的艾莉明显有年龄差，且像同一个人
- [ ] 手指没有变形，蛋糕和礼物结构正常

## 三、四季与夜晚房间（图生图，5 项 × 2 候选）

### 3.1 为什么必须用图生图

主界面的女儿立绘是单独叠在房间背景上的，脚底对准房间中央的粉色地毯。**家具、地毯、窗户和墙面的位置一旦变化，人物就会“站错地方”**。所以这 5 张必须以现有房间为底图，只改季节相关的部分。

- **底图**：`art-src/stargarden/production-v1/room-portrait_2.png`（1024×1536，竖版）
- **输出尺寸**：与底图完全相同，1024×1536，不要裁切、不要改比例
- **画面里不出现任何人物**

### 3.2 推荐做法（按可用工具选择）

1. **局部重绘（首选）**：只给窗户区域（含窗帘）和需要加季节小物的位置画遮罩，其余区域保持原图。季节变化主要在窗外，这样最稳。
2. **整图图生图**：工具不支持遮罩时，用较低的重绘强度（约 0.25–0.4，以工具说明为准），并在提示词里强调构图不变；强度太高家具会移位，太低季节变化不明显，需要试两三档。
3. **夜晚**需要整体调暗、加灯光，局部重绘不够时可用整图图生图，强度稍高（约 0.4–0.5），重点检查家具是否移位。

### 3.3 不能改的（每张都要写进提示词）

```
Keep the exact same room composition as the input image: same camera angle, same perspective, same positions and shapes of the window, curtains, writing desk and chair, bookshelf, table lamp, wardrobe, wall shelf, framed picture, hanging dried flowers, wooden floor planks and the dusty rose oval rug in the center. Do not move, add or remove furniture. Keep the center of the room clear. No people. Keep the same 1990s Japanese cel animation style with fine brown outlines and flat colors.
```

### 3.4 每张要改的

| 编号 | 季节（游戏月份） | 提示词（接在 3.3 之后） |
|---|---|---|
| `home-room-spring` | 春（2–4 月） | `Spring version: outside the window, soft pale green new leaves and pink and white blossoming fruit trees under a clear light blue sky with small white clouds. A few cherry-blossom petals drifting near the window. On the desk, a small glass jar with fresh spring flowers. Bright fresh morning light.` |
| `home-room-summer` | 夏（5–7 月） | `Summer version: outside the window, lush deep green trees and a bright saturated blue sky with tall white cumulus clouds. The window slightly open, curtains lifted by a light breeze, a small glass wind chime hanging at the window frame. A bowl of cherries on the desk. Strong clear sunlight with crisp shadows on the floor.` |
| `home-room-autumn` | 秋（8–10 月） | `Autumn version: outside the window, trees in warm orange, amber and red, a few falling leaves, a soft golden afternoon sky. On the desk, a small basket with apples and a few red maple leaves. Warm golden light slanting across the floor.` |
| `home-room-winter` | 冬（11–1 月） | `Winter version: outside the window, snow-covered rooftops and trees under a pale winter sky, gentle falling snow, a little frost at the corners of the window glass. A soft knitted cream blanket folded over the desk chair, a steaming mug on the desk. Cool bright daylight inside, cozy and warm, not gloomy.` |
| `home-room-night` | 夜晚（谈心、夜间事件） | `Night version: outside the window, a deep navy starry night sky with a crescent moon and a few warm town lights in the distance. The table lamp on the bookshelf is switched on, casting a warm amber glow over the room. The room is dimmer than daytime but still clearly readable, about 70 percent of daytime brightness, soft warm tones, no harsh darkness, no heavy vignette.` |

> 夜晚版亮度不能太暗：游戏里女儿立绘是白天光照，背景太暗会显得人物像贴上去的。

### 3.5 验收（逐张）

- [ ] 尺寸 1024×1536，与底图一致
- [ ] **构图对齐**：把成品与底图叠在一起（50% 透明度或做差值图）检查，家具、地毯、窗框、墙面装饰的位置和轮廓基本重合，没有明显移位、变形或增减
- [ ] 地毯中心位置不变（人物站位）
- [ ] 季节特征明确，一眼能分出春夏秋冬
- [ ] 画风与底图一致，没有变成写实或厚涂
- [ ] 没有人物、文字、水印
- [ ] 夜晚版房间可看清，暖光自然

### 3.6 用途（供程序侧参考）

- 主界面按季节切换房间背景；谈心与夜间事件使用夜晚版。
- 衣橱预览、事件叠层画面同样按季节使用。
- 作为 AI 视频“时光流逝”转场路线 B 的首尾帧（见 `AI视频需求与提示词.md` 第五节），以及“庭院窗外动态”其余三季的首帧（第六节）。
